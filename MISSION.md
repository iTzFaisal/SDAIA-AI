# Mission: AI Engineer Expert Badge

## Why

Help people who are preparing for the AI Engineer Expert badge turn the supplied exam blueprint into durable, production-oriented capability. The immediate outcome is exam readiness; the deeper outcome is being able to explain and defend the decisions involved in building and operating AI systems.

## Success looks like

- Explain the seven exam domains and connect them across an AI system lifecycle.
- Choose appropriate data practices, models, evaluation methods, architectures, and operational controls for a scenario.
- Build and defend a small document-intelligence/RAG service with a classical-ML component.
- Correctly reason through original domain questions and timed mixed-domain mocks.
- Reach 80% in every domain and 85% on two complete timed mocks.

## Constraints

- Twelve weeks at 6-8 hours per week.
- English teaching materials.
- Broad audience with a day-one diagnostic and adaptive routes.
- Local-first, cloud-neutral practical work.
- The supplied `AI-Badge.png` is the authoritative exam blueprint for this workspace.
- Lessons should be short, interactive, and supported by high-trust sources.

## Out of scope

- Registration, eligibility, payment, test-center, or badge-receipt logistics.
- Leaked, reconstructed, or claimed-authentic exam questions.
- Vendor-specific certification preparation that is not needed for the seven domains.
- Broad career coaching unrelated to passing the exam.
