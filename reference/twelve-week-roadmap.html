Default commitment

6-8 hours per week

Use four required sessions and one optional session. If a learner is on the Foundation route, spend the optional session on prerequisites. If on the Accelerated route, spend it on mixed-domain scenarios or capstone defense.

Rule: do not advance because the calendar says so. Advance when the learner can explain the decision, complete the practical task, and repair the most important error.

Capstone spine

Keep one system in view

The document-intelligence/RAG service is deliberately small. It gives every domain a concrete place to land without turning exam preparation into a large product build.

Input

Documents and questions

Define provenance, access rules, and a useful outcome before choosing a model.

Decision

Ground or generate

Compare retrieval, prompting, fine-tuning, and a classical-ML component against the risk.

Operation

Measure and govern

Track quality, latency, cost, safety, drift, incidents, and accountability after release.
