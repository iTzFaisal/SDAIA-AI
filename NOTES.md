# Teaching Notes

- The audience is broad: do not assume one professional background beyond basic Python, algebra/probability, Git, and command-line use.
- Teach in English.
- Target the AI Engineer Expert badge, not the lower badge levels.
- Use the supplied `AI-Badge.png` as the source of truth for the exam blueprint.
- Keep administrative logistics out of the course.
- Prefer local-first, cloud-neutral labs with optional cloud mappings.
- Use original practice questions only; never imply access to live or leaked items.
- Teach interactively, one short lesson at a time, and use demonstrated evidence to choose what comes next.
- Keep the production-style capstone in service of exam readiness rather than allowing it to become a separate software project.
