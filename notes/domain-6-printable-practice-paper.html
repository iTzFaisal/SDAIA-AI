24 original questions; one best answer each. Suggested practice time: 45 minutes, an unofficial training choice. Write your answers separately. Unanswered questions count as incorrect. The answer key is in a separate file. This is not a full seven-domain mock. No answers appear in this paper.

1. Which statement best connects SDAIA's published ethics principles to AI development?

  1. They matter only after a public complaint
  2. Fairness alone replaces privacy and safety
  3. Listing them certifies official exam readiness
  4. They guide concrete controls from planning through deployment and monitoring

2. A model is trained to predict past loan approval, and historical approvals excluded qualified applicants from one region. What should be investigated first?

  1. Whether deeper layers can copy approval history more accurately
  2. Whether the label represents a defensible desired outcome and whether coverage is adequate
  3. Whether more duplicate approval records will guarantee fairness
  4. Whether deleting the region name alone eliminates all related influence

3. In a fictional screen, Group A has 12 false positives among 60 actual negatives; Group B has 12 among 30. What are their false-positive rates?

  1. A 20%; B 40%
  2. Both 12% because counts match
  3. A 40%; B 20%
  4. A 80%; B 60%

4. Two groups have equal selection rates but different recall, prevalence, and error consequences. What is the most defensible fairness conclusion?

  1. Equal selection proves every aspect of fairness
  2. One threshold must make every fairness metric equal
  3. Inspect valid labels, counts, uncertainty, consequences, and contextual tradeoffs
  4. Tune repeatedly on the reserved test until every rate matches

5. What is the best distinction between a model card and a system-level record?

  1. Both guarantee legal compliance regardless of application
  2. A model card must reveal all production secrets
  3. The system record also covers the model's retrieval, tools, users, oversight, and downstream use
  4. Neither needs to match the deployed version

6. A feature-contribution report says commuting distance influenced a hiring prediction. What must not be inferred from that alone?

  1. Moving closer will causally guarantee hiring
  2. The explanation concerns a model result
  3. The actual input and model version should be checked
  4. The audience needs an understandable account

7. A team must review 160 decisions/hour. Each takes 2 minutes and four reviewers work at the ideal rate. What is the capacity gap?

  1. No gap; four reviewers complete 240/hour
  2. Only 4 decisions/hour can be reviewed
  3. 80 decisions/hour of spare capacity
  4. Capacity 120/hour; backlog grows 40/hour before other overhead

8. An appeal button asks the same unchecked model to repeat its original recommendation. What is the strongest improvement?

  2. Route to qualified review with source evidence and authority to correct or reverse the decision
  3. Hide disagreement so users cannot be confused
  4. Publish other applicants' records to explain the ranking
