## 1. Residents repeatedly abandon a permit form. Which statement is the strongest problem-first framing?

Topic: Discover the problem & earn the use of AI. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. We need the largest available language model:** This prescribes a technical solution without establishing the cause of abandonment.
- **B. Help residents submit a valid permit application by identifying the steps causing abandonment — correct:** It names an affected population, desired outcome, and obstacle that research can investigate.
- **C. Every resident needs a chatbot regardless of evidence:** A solution assumption is not a verified need shared by every user.
- **D. Maximize the number of generated paragraphs:** Output volume does not measure whether residents complete valid applications.

## 2. A stable policy gives an explicit fee for each permit type. Authorized data is sparse. What is the most defensible initial comparison?

Topic: Discover the problem & earn the use of AI. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. Train a large model because rules are never useful:** Fixed explicit requirements are a strong reason to consider a deterministic baseline.
- **B. Assume more synthetic examples prove real-world generalization:** Synthetic volume does not establish representative evidence about actual users and cases.
- **C. Choose AI before defining fee errors or maintenance needs:** Error costs and changing-policy maintenance matter for every alternative.
- **D. Compare an explicit rule or lookup with the current process before earning ML complexity — correct:** A simple alternative may directly meet the need; complexity must demonstrate added value.

## 3. A proposed assistant has excellent quality and low cost, but its required secondary use of personal data has not been approved or justified. What should happen?

Topic: Discover the problem & earn the use of AI. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. Hold that processing while qualified owners establish a permitted purpose, basis, and controls — correct:** Technical and economic evidence do not pass the separate legal and privacy feasibility gate.
- **B. Proceed because positive ROI authorizes the purpose:** A financial calculation cannot create legal permission for processing.
- **C. Use encryption and assume all secondary uses are allowed:** Encryption reduces exposure but does not establish purpose or legal basis.
- **D. Let the vendor's marketing label determine controller duties:** Actual purposes, uses, and applicable rules determine duties, not a supplier label.

## 4. A bounded pilot with trained volunteers succeeds. The sponsor proposes immediate release to every branch with no support changes. Which conclusion is best?

Topic: Discover the problem & earn the use of AI. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. Pilot success proves all branches are covered:** Different users, cases, data, and support conditions can invalidate that generalization.
- **B. The pilot must be discarded because volunteers participated:** The evidence can be useful within its scope without proving the broad release.
- **C. Use the pilot as scoped evidence, then assess other populations, support, risks, and release gates — correct:** A pilot narrows uncertainty but does not automatically authorize wider production use.
- **D. Remove fallback to make adoption appear higher:** Forced use and missing fallback can increase harm without demonstrating benefit.

## 5. A team measures daily generated-answer count as its only success metric for customer support. What is the main problem?

Topic: Define success, total cost & business value. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. Counts can never be useful:** Counts are useful with scope and outcomes; they simply cannot establish success alone.
- **B. Any high count guarantees customer satisfaction:** High activity can coexist with wrong answers, repeated contacts, and unresolved needs.
- **C. Activity is not verified resolution; outcomes, denominators, cost, and harm guardrails are needed — correct:** Success should connect to the user's completed task rather than output production alone.
- **D. Replacing count with parameter size solves the issue:** Model size is even further from the desired customer outcome.

## 6. A workflow saves a net 4 minutes on each of 900 monthly cases after review/rework. At SAR 50/hour of capacity value, what benefit is estimated?

Topic: Define success, total cost & business value. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. 60 hours and SAR 3,000/month of capacity value, not proven cash savings — correct:** 900 × 4/60 = 60 hours; 60 × 50 = 3,000. Monetized capacity does not show payroll reduction.
- **B. 3,600 hours and SAR 180,000 of guaranteed cash savings:** 3,600 is minutes, and the benefit type is capacity value rather than established cash.
- **C. 15 hours because case volume must be divided by 60 first and minutes ignored:** The per-case net minutes must multiply the case count before conversion to hours.
- **D. SAR 3,000 of net profit regardless of project costs:** This is gross capacity benefit; costs are still needed to calculate net benefit.

## 7. Setup costs SAR 18,000. Constant monthly incremental cash benefit is SAR 5,000 and operating cost is SAR 2,000 after launch. Under the stated simple convention, what is payback?

Topic: Define success, total cost & business value. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. 3.6 months:** 18,000/5,000 omits the monthly operating cost, so it is not net-benefit payback.
- **B. 9 months:** Dividing by operating cost uses the wrong quantity; net monthly benefit is 3,000.
- **C. Immediate because monthly benefits exceed operating costs:** Positive ongoing net benefit still needs time to recover setup spending.
- **D. 6 months after launch under these constant assumptions — correct:** Recurring net benefit is 5,000 − 2,000 = 3,000; 18,000/3,000 = 6 months.

## 8. A monthly service has SAR 8,000 fixed incremental cost and SAR 4 net contribution per completed task after all variable costs. What is monthly break-even volume?

Topic: Define success, total cost & business value. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. 32,000 tasks:** Multiplying fixed cost by contribution does not produce the required volume.
- **B. 2,000 completed tasks/month — correct:** 8,000/4 = 2,000; contribution is already net of variable costs for the same monthly period.
- **C. 2,000 tasks/year:** This changes the period even though the fixed cost is explicitly monthly.
- **D. No finite volume even with positive contribution:** With the stated positive contribution and constant assumptions, a finite break-even exists.

## 9. Which distinction is correct when presenting an AI pilot's results?

Topic: Communicate evidence, uncertainty & tradeoffs. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. A model confidence score is automatically the probability that the business case will succeed:** Model scoring and uncertainty about business value concern different claims.
- **B. A vendor benchmark is our measured result if the model name matches:** Local populations, workflows, versions, and evaluation methods can differ.
- **C. A forecast becomes measured evidence once a sponsor approves it:** Approval does not change an assumption-based estimate into an observed outcome.
- **D. Measured pilot outcomes, forecasts, and unknown deployment conditions should be labeled separately — correct:** Keeping evidence types distinct prevents unsupported guarantees and guides the next decision.

## 10. Among 300 eligible requests, 180 receive answers and 144 answers pass review. Which rates describe this result?

Topic: Communicate evidence, uncertainty & tradeoffs. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. 80% coverage and 60% correctness among answers:** Coverage uses 180/300, while answer correctness uses 144/180; these are reversed.
- **B. 80% success across all eligible requests:** 80% is correctness among answers; only 144 of 300 eligible requests received passing answers.
- **C. 60% coverage; 80% of answers pass; 48% of eligible requests receive a passing answer — correct:** 180/300 = 60%, 144/180 = 80%, and 144/300 = 48%, each with its stated denominator.
- **D. 100% coverage if unanswered cases are removed:** The eligible cohort includes unanswered requests and must not disappear from coverage.

## 11. Correct completion rises from 40% to 50% after several staffing and software changes. What is the defensible summary?

Topic: Communicate evidence, uncertainty & tradeoffs. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. AI caused a 10% relative improvement:** The relative change is 25%, and the before/after comparison cannot isolate AI's effect.
- **B. A 10-percentage-point rise, or 25% relative increase; AI causation is not established — correct:** 50 − 40 = 10 points and 10/40 = 25%; other changes are potential explanations.
- **C. A 25-percentage-point rise with guaranteed future performance:** The point change is 10, and the historical comparison does not guarantee future outcomes.
- **D. No improvement can be reported unless every cause is known:** The observed change can be described honestly without claiming a specific cause.

## 12. Overall pilot quality improves, but a small unrepresentative sample contains a serious harmful failure. How should a sponsor brief handle it?

Topic: Communicate evidence, uncertainty & tradeoffs. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. Report the improvement and failure with scope limits, investigate harm, and recommend bounded next steps — correct:** A favorable average does not justify hiding serious failures or unsupported generalization.
- **B. Omit the failure because the average rose:** The failure may affect an essential harm guardrail despite average improvement.
- **C. Claim the small sample proves zero risk in untested groups:** Limited biased evidence cannot establish safety in populations it does not cover.
- **D. Include every raw private record to appear transparent:** Transparency should use proportionate protected evidence, not unnecessary disclosure.

## 13. Who should define whether a case counts as a genuinely resolved support issue?

Topic: Collaborate through ownership, review & handoffs. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. Domain and service stakeholders with users and evaluators, using a shared documented rubric — correct:** Meaningful outcomes require domain understanding and shared criteria, not a model-only definition.
- **B. Only the supplier's sales representative:** Commercial expertise does not supply the full local outcome definition or independence.
- **C. Whichever person owns the largest GPU:** Hardware ownership is unrelated to the validity of the business outcome rubric.
- **D. Nobody; closed-ticket status is always perfect ground truth:** A closed ticket may not mean the customer's problem was actually solved.

## 14. A simple responsibility matrix names a release decision owner. Privacy approval is separately required for the proposed data use. What follows?

Topic: Collaborate through ownership, review & handoffs. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. The release owner can waive the privacy approval:** Collaboration accountability does not override a required approval gate.
- **B. Being consulted automatically makes privacy owners approve:** Consultation supplies input but does not establish affirmative approval.
- **C. Keep a clear decision owner while preserving and documenting the required privacy gate — correct:** RACI helps coordination without replacing legal or organizational approval responsibilities.
- **D. Remove the owner so nobody is accountable:** Removing accountability makes the decision less clear rather than resolving the requirement.

## 15. Which handoff package best enables a receiving team to support a retrieval assistant?

Topic: Collaborate through ownership, review & handoffs. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. Only a chart of training accuracy:** Training accuracy does not provide compatible versions, runtime limits, or recovery procedures.
- **B. Permitted scope, release/source versions, evidence/gaps, access, alerts, runbook, contacts, and rehearsed acceptance — correct:** This supplies the information and verified capabilities needed for accepted operational ownership.
- **C. Passwords pasted into an unrestricted presentation:** Credentials must use approved access processes, not unsafe document disclosure.
- **D. An unowned folder with several contradictory runbooks:** Conflicting unmanaged documents are not a dependable source of operating instructions.

## 16. A project lead and evaluator disagree about launching with missing delayed outcomes. What is the strongest next action?

Topic: Collaborate through ownership, review & handoffs. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. Assume missing outcomes are successful to settle the disagreement:** Unknown outcomes are not successes and cannot be fabricated for consensus.
- **B. Use the project lead's seniority as performance evidence:** Authority can make a permitted decision but cannot manufacture missing evidence.
- **C. Vote to remove the evaluation finding from the record:** Concealing the gap prevents accountable decision-making and useful later review.
- **D. Document evidence, impact, options and gates; seek the authorized decision with unresolved limits explicit — correct:** This separates the factual gap from decision authority while preserving necessary release constraints.

## 17. What is opportunity cost when one of two competing projects uses all available reviewer capacity?

Topic: Prioritize work & manage adoption and change. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. Only the chosen project's cloud invoice:** Opportunity cost concerns the forgone alternative, not just a direct bill.
- **B. The value of the best alternative forgone by committing that capacity — correct:** Scarce reviewers cannot simultaneously deliver both alternatives; the lost option matters.
- **C. A guarantee that the chosen project is profitable:** Considering opportunity cost does not establish either project's actual benefits.
- **D. The number of parameters in both models:** Parameter counts do not measure the value lost by the resource commitment.

## 18. Ten review-days are available. A required safety repair needs three, and two independent permitted improvements each need four. What can the team responsibly commit now?

Topic: Prioritize work & manage adoption and change. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. The required repair and at most one full improvement within the remaining seven days — correct:** After 3 days, 7 remain; two 4-day improvements need 8 and exceed the stated capacity.
- **B. Both improvements by skipping the required repair:** Optional value scoring cannot justify bypassing the required safety work.
- **C. All three tasks within ten days without changing scope or capacity:** 3 + 4 + 4 = 11 days, so that commitment contradicts the stated constraint.
- **D. Nothing, because prioritization cannot ever use estimates:** Estimates can support an honest bounded commitment while retaining their uncertainty.

## 19. A drafting service sees low regular use despite high training attendance. What should change management investigate?

Topic: Prioritize work & manage adoption and change. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. Only whether staff can be forced to accept every draft:** Forced uncritical use can worsen harm and does not establish appropriate adoption.
- **B. How to remove nonusers from the eligible denominator:** Changing the denominator to hide low use misrepresents adoption.
- **C. Whether calling the prototype production changes outcomes:** A label does not fix integration friction, support, or unmet needs.
- **D. Workflow friction, accessibility, net review burden, user needs, and actual completed outcomes — correct:** Low uptake can reveal product or workflow problems that training attendance alone misses.

## 20. An organization plans to retire a policy assistant that several teams depend on. Which plan is most responsible?

Topic: Prioritize work & manage adoption and change. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. Delete the interface immediately and assume all data obligations disappear:** Removing a UI neither supports dependent users nor removes retained copies or legal duties.
- **B. Keep an unsupported service forever regardless of risk:** Continuing unsafe unsupported operation is not automatically a responsible alternative.
- **C. Map dependencies, communicate migration, provide a workable alternative, and manage access/data/support through transition — correct:** Careful retirement protects dependent workflows while preserving applicable obligations.
- **D. Inform only the original developer after shutdown:** Affected users, operators, and integration owners need timely transition information.

## 21. A manager asks an evaluator to remove difficult eligible cases without disclosure so a release chart improves. What is the best response?

Topic: Practice integrity, escalation & continuous learning. Practice difficulty: Foundation (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: C.**

- **A. Comply because deadlines authorize favorable evidence:** A deadline does not justify misleading cohort selection or falsification.
- **B. Count the excluded cases as correct without reviewing them:** Unknown or excluded outcomes cannot honestly become successes.
- **C. Preserve honest cohort definitions, document the concern, and escalate through appropriate owners if needed — correct:** Integrity requires accurate reporting and a usable response to pressure that would mislead decision-makers.
- **D. Publish confidential customer cases publicly to punish the manager:** Unnecessary disclosure and retaliation add harm instead of enabling an appropriate protected escalation.

## 22. An engineer wants to reuse a publicly downloadable model and dataset in a commercial proposal. What must they check?

Topic: Practice integrity, escalation & continuous learning. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: B.**

- **A. Only whether downloads are fast:** Download speed provides no evidence about permission, licensing, or fitness.
- **B. Licenses, permissions, provenance, attribution, and suitability for the intended use — correct:** Public access alone does not establish reuse rights or technical and ethical suitability.
- **C. Whether renaming files removes all license conditions:** Renaming artifacts does not erase applicable terms or origin.
- **D. Nothing if a colleague already downloaded them:** Possession by someone else does not establish authorized use for the proposal.

## 23. A security concern is reported vaguely as 'the AI may be unsafe.' Which addition most improves the escalation?

Topic: Practice integrity, escalation & continuous learning. Practice difficulty: Applied (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: A.**

- **A. Observed behavior and versions, affected scope, protected evidence, containment, uncertainty, owner, and requested action — correct:** Concrete proportionate facts and a decision request help authorized responders act promptly.
- **B. Every secret in a public chat so more people can help:** Broad uncontrolled disclosure can expose assets and worsen the incident.
- **C. A claim of confirmed poisoning despite no cause evidence:** Hypotheses should be identified as uncertain rather than presented as established facts.
- **D. A demand to wait until every forensic detail is complete:** Potential material harm can require prompt escalation while investigation continues.

## 24. A post-incident review ends with 'staff will learn more about AI.' What makes the corrective work verifiable?

Topic: Practice integrity, escalation & continuous learning. Practice difficulty: Stretch (not an official question level).

**Correct answer: D.**

- **A. Blame one individual and skip workflow analysis:** Contributing factors can include incentives, access design, tests, and unclear ownership.
- **B. Count opening a training link as proof the incident cannot recur:** Attendance or link access does not verify improved capability or corrected controls.
- **C. Retake the identical quiz until everyone memorizes the answers:** Familiar-question improvement cannot establish new-case performance or system fixes.
- **D. Specify corrective action, owner, due date, and fresh tests of the actual failure boundary — correct:** Verification must show the control and process improved, with accountable follow-through rather than vague intent.
