### 1. Residents repeatedly abandon a permit form. Which statement is the strongest problem-first framing?

A. We need the largest available language model

B. Help residents submit a valid permit application by identifying the steps causing abandonment

C. Every resident needs a chatbot regardless of evidence

D. Maximize the number of generated paragraphs

### 2. A stable policy gives an explicit fee for each permit type. Authorized data is sparse. What is the most defensible initial comparison?

A. Train a large model because rules are never useful

B. Assume more synthetic examples prove real-world generalization

C. Choose AI before defining fee errors or maintenance needs

D. Compare an explicit rule or lookup with the current process before earning ML complexity

### 3. A proposed assistant has excellent quality and low cost, but its required secondary use of personal data has not been approved or justified. What should happen?

A. Hold that processing while qualified owners establish a permitted purpose, basis, and controls

B. Proceed because positive ROI authorizes the purpose

C. Use encryption and assume all secondary uses are allowed

D. Let the vendor's marketing label determine controller duties

### 4. A bounded pilot with trained volunteers succeeds. The sponsor proposes immediate release to every branch with no support changes. Which conclusion is best?

A. Pilot success proves all branches are covered

B. The pilot must be discarded because volunteers participated

C. Use the pilot as scoped evidence, then assess other populations, support, risks, and release gates

D. Remove fallback to make adoption appear higher

### 5. A team measures daily generated-answer count as its only success metric for customer support. What is the main problem?

A. Counts can never be useful

B. Any high count guarantees customer satisfaction

C. Activity is not verified resolution; outcomes, denominators, cost, and harm guardrails are needed

D. Replacing count with parameter size solves the issue

### 6. A workflow saves a net 4 minutes on each of 900 monthly cases after review/rework. At SAR 50/hour of capacity value, what benefit is estimated?

A. 60 hours and SAR 3,000/month of capacity value, not proven cash savings

B. 3,600 hours and SAR 180,000 of guaranteed cash savings

C. 15 hours because case volume must be divided by 60 first and minutes ignored

D. SAR 3,000 of net profit regardless of project costs

### 7. Setup costs SAR 18,000. Constant monthly incremental cash benefit is SAR 5,000 and operating cost is SAR 2,000 after launch. Under the stated simple convention, what is payback?

A. 3.6 months

B. 9 months

C. Immediate because monthly benefits exceed operating costs

D. 6 months after launch under these constant assumptions

### 8. A monthly service has SAR 8,000 fixed incremental cost and SAR 4 net contribution per completed task after all variable costs. What is monthly break-even volume?

A. 32,000 tasks

B. 2,000 completed tasks/month

C. 2,000 tasks/year

D. No finite volume even with positive contribution

### 9. Which distinction is correct when presenting an AI pilot's results?

A. A model confidence score is automatically the probability that the business case will succeed

B. A vendor benchmark is our measured result if the model name matches

C. A forecast becomes measured evidence once a sponsor approves it

D. Measured pilot outcomes, forecasts, and unknown deployment conditions should be labeled separately

### 10. Among 300 eligible requests, 180 receive answers and 144 answers pass review. Which rates describe this result?

A. 80% coverage and 60% correctness among answers

B. 80% success across all eligible requests

C. 60% coverage; 80% of answers pass; 48% of eligible requests receive a passing answer

D. 100% coverage if unanswered cases are removed

### 11. Correct completion rises from 40% to 50% after several staffing and software changes. What is the defensible summary?

A. AI caused a 10% relative improvement

B. A 10-percentage-point rise, or 25% relative increase; AI causation is not established

C. A 25-percentage-point rise with guaranteed future performance

D. No improvement can be reported unless every cause is known

### 12. Overall pilot quality improves, but a small unrepresentative sample contains a serious harmful failure. How should a sponsor brief handle it?

A. Report the improvement and failure with scope limits, investigate harm, and recommend bounded next steps

B. Omit the failure because the average rose

C. Claim the small sample proves zero risk in untested groups

D. Include every raw private record to appear transparent

### 13. Who should define whether a case counts as a genuinely resolved support issue?

A. Domain and service stakeholders with users and evaluators, using a shared documented rubric

B. Only the supplier's sales representative

C. Whichever person owns the largest GPU

D. Nobody; closed-ticket status is always perfect ground truth

### 14. A simple responsibility matrix names a release decision owner. Privacy approval is separately required for the proposed data use. What follows?

A. The release owner can waive the privacy approval

B. Being consulted automatically makes privacy owners approve

C. Keep a clear decision owner while preserving and documenting the required privacy gate

D. Remove the owner so nobody is accountable

### 15. Which handoff package best enables a receiving team to support a retrieval assistant?

A. Only a chart of training accuracy

B. Permitted scope, release/source versions, evidence/gaps, access, alerts, runbook, contacts, and rehearsed acceptance

C. Passwords pasted into an unrestricted presentation

D. An unowned folder with several contradictory runbooks

### 16. A project lead and evaluator disagree about launching with missing delayed outcomes. What is the strongest next action?

A. Assume missing outcomes are successful to settle the disagreement

B. Use the project lead's seniority as performance evidence

C. Vote to remove the evaluation finding from the record

D. Document evidence, impact, options and gates; seek the authorized decision with unresolved limits explicit

### 17. What is opportunity cost when one of two competing projects uses all available reviewer capacity?

A. Only the chosen project's cloud invoice

B. The value of the best alternative forgone by committing that capacity

C. A guarantee that the chosen project is profitable

D. The number of parameters in both models

### 18. Ten review-days are available. A required safety repair needs three, and two independent permitted improvements each need four. What can the team responsibly commit now?

A. The required repair and at most one full improvement within the remaining seven days

B. Both improvements by skipping the required repair

C. All three tasks within ten days without changing scope or capacity

D. Nothing, because prioritization cannot ever use estimates

### 19. A drafting service sees low regular use despite high training attendance. What should change management investigate?

A. Only whether staff can be forced to accept every draft

B. How to remove nonusers from the eligible denominator

C. Whether calling the prototype production changes outcomes

D. Workflow friction, accessibility, net review burden, user needs, and actual completed outcomes

### 20. An organization plans to retire a policy assistant that several teams depend on. Which plan is most responsible?

A. Delete the interface immediately and assume all data obligations disappear

B. Keep an unsupported service forever regardless of risk

C. Map dependencies, communicate migration, provide a workable alternative, and manage access/data/support through transition

D. Inform only the original developer after shutdown

### 21. A manager asks an evaluator to remove difficult eligible cases without disclosure so a release chart improves. What is the best response?

A. Comply because deadlines authorize favorable evidence

B. Count the excluded cases as correct without reviewing them

C. Preserve honest cohort definitions, document the concern, and escalate through appropriate owners if needed

D. Publish confidential customer cases publicly to punish the manager

### 22. An engineer wants to reuse a publicly downloadable model and dataset in a commercial proposal. What must they check?

A. Only whether downloads are fast

B. Licenses, permissions, provenance, attribution, and suitability for the intended use

C. Whether renaming files removes all license conditions

D. Nothing if a colleague already downloaded them

### 23. A security concern is reported vaguely as 'the AI may be unsafe.' Which addition most improves the escalation?

A. Observed behavior and versions, affected scope, protected evidence, containment, uncertainty, owner, and requested action

B. Every secret in a public chat so more people can help

C. A claim of confirmed poisoning despite no cause evidence

D. A demand to wait until every forensic detail is complete

A. Blame one individual and skip workflow analysis

B. Count opening a training link as proof the incident cannot recur

C. Retake the identical quiz until everyone memorizes the answers

D. Specify corrective action, owner, due date, and fresh tests of the actual failure boundary
